Studio

We design a few buildings a year, slowly and completely — houses, hotels and workplaces where light, material and silence do the work.

Atelier Noor was founded in Lisbon in 2011 by Inês Carvalho and Karim Haddad, and opened its Dubai studio in 2017. We take on eight to ten commissions at a time and see every one through from the first site visit to the last door handle.

Practice

What we do

  1. 01

    Architecture

    New buildings and the careful remaking of old ones, from concept and planning through to construction.

    Houses · Restorations · Extensions

  2. 02

    Interiors

    Rooms designed together with the building around them: joinery, lighting, furniture and the materials you touch.

    Residential · Collections · Joinery

  3. 03

    Hospitality

    Small hotels, guesthouses and restaurants for operators who measure success by return visits rather than room count.

    Hotels · Restaurants · Residencies

  4. 04

    Workplace

    Studios and offices for organisations that make things — calm, daylit spaces where people choose to spend the day.

    Studios · Family offices · Libraries
