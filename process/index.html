Pl. 08

From brief to handover

Every commission follows the same five steps. A house typically takes eighteen months to two years from first visit to keys; a hotel, three to four.

  1. 01

    Brief & site

    4–6 weeks

    We visit the site at different times of day, measure what is there and listen. The output is a written brief we agree together — what the building must do, what it must never do, and what it may cost.

    • Measured survey
    • Daylight study
    • Written brief
    • Budget range
  2. 02

    Concept

    6–8 weeks

    Two or three distinct directions, each drawn by hand and modelled at 1:100. We choose one with you, in the studio, around the models — never from a screen.

    • Concept drawings
    • Physical models
    • Material direction
    • Cost check
  3. 03

    Design development

    3–5 months

    The chosen direction becomes a building: structure, services, planning drawings and the first samples of stone, timber and plaster on site, in real light.

    • Planning application
    • Engineer coordination
    • Sample panels
    • Detailed cost plan
  4. 04

    Detail & tender

    3–4 months

    Every junction, joint and handle is drawn — typically 400 to 900 drawings for a house. We tender to builders we have worked with before and help you choose.

    • Construction drawings
    • Specifications
    • Tender analysis
    • Contract
  5. 05

    Construction & handover

    9–18 months

    We are on site every week. At the end we hand over the keys, the drawings as built, a maintenance guide written in plain language — and we come back after the first year.

    • Weekly site visits
    • Snagging
    • As-built drawings
    • One-year return visit

Questions

Often asked

How do your fees work?+

For most projects we charge a fixed fee per stage, agreed in writing before each stage begins. As a guide, full services on a house are typically 12–15% of construction cost.

Do you work outside Portugal and the UAE?+

Occasionally, when a project is right for us. We have completed buildings in Spain, Morocco and Oman, always with a local architect of record.

What is the smallest project you take on?+

We rarely start projects with a construction budget below €400,000 or AED 1.6 million, because we can't give smaller projects the time they deserve.

Can we commission interiors only?+

Yes — about a third of our work is interiors in buildings designed by others, from single apartments to hotel floors.
