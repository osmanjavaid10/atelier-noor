Pl. 07

The studio

Twenty-three architects, interior designers and makers in two cities, working as one practice on a small number of buildings at a time.

Philosophy

Three things we believe

  1. i.

    Light is the first material.

    Before we choose a stone or a timber we study where the sun falls, at what hour, in which month. A room is designed around the light it will receive; everything else follows.

  2. ii.

    Keep what is good.

    Most of our work begins with an existing building. We repair before we replace, and when we add something new we let it look new, so a building can be read honestly for another century.

  3. iii.

    Fewer, finished things.

    We take on eight to ten commissions at a time so that the people who draw a building are the people who see it built. Nothing leaves the studio half-resolved — including the door handles.

Partners and associates

  • Rui Mendonça

    Associate, architecture

    Leads site work in Lisbon. Believes the first site visit is the most important day of any project.

  • Sofia Marques

    Senior architect

    Moved between the two studios for The Courtyard House. Specialises in daylight modelling.

  • Tomás Reis

    Architect

    Joined in 2020. Builds the studio's physical models — around forty a year, at 1:50 and 1:20.

Offices

Two studios, one practice

Both studios work on every project. Drawings move between Lisbon and Dubai overnight, so a design is quietly advanced around the clock.
