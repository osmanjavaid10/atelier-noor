Rua do Século Studios

The timber screen around the reading room.

Location
Bairro Alto, Lisbon
Year
2023
Area
1,180 m²
Status
Completed
Client
Século Type Foundry & Press
Desks
38 drawing desks, 4 presses
Daylight
North-lit sawtooth roof

A type foundry, a letterpress and a reading room under one timber roof, lit from above so that no desk faces a window.

The brief

A type foundry and its sister letterpress studio had outgrown three separate rented floors. They bought a 1950s print works on Rua do Século — one large hall with a failing roof — and asked for a single place where drawing, printing and reading could happen side by side without disturbing one another.

Glare on screens and on wet ink was the foundry's first complaint about every office they had ever had.

Our approach

We replaced the roof with a sawtooth of glued-laminated larch, each ridge glazed to the north. Daylight arrives evenly, all day, from above; the side walls are left blind and lined with shelving for the foundry's archive of 14,000 specimens.

The hall is zoned by sound rather than walls. The presses sit at the street end on an isolated slab; drawing desks occupy the middle; the reading room at the rear is wrapped in a curved timber screen that softens everything behind it.
