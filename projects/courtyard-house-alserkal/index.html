The Courtyard House

The courtyard pool at dusk, reflecting the east arcade.

Location
Al Quoz, Dubai
Year
2025
Area
1,620 m²
Status
Completed
Client
Sikka Hospitality
Keys
14 studio rooms
Stays
From 7 nights to 3 months

A fourteen-key guesthouse for visiting artists, built around a still pool and a wall that the afternoon light paints for an hour each day.

The brief

The arts district had galleries, studios and cafés, but nowhere for the artists themselves to stay. The client wanted a guesthouse that felt like a residency rather than a hotel — quiet, unbranded, and a short walk from the warehouses.

The plot is a former vehicle yard between two warehouses, with no outward view worth having.

Our approach

With nothing to look out at, the house looks in. Fourteen rooms wrap a single courtyard on two levels; the courtyard holds a 21-metre pool with a black basalt floor, so the water reads as a mirror rather than a swimming pool.

The west wall of the courtyard is a single plane of fluted render, nine metres tall. We studied its profile for a summer: at 4:50 pm in July the fluting turns the wall to gold for about an hour, and the house was planned so that the shared table looks straight at it.

Rooms are fitted like studios — a long work table, a pin wall, north light — and can be booked for a month at a time.
