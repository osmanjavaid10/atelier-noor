Casa do Miradouro

The Lioz stair at four in the afternoon, lit from the roof light above.

Year
2024

412 m²
Status
Completed
Client
Private client
Floors
4 + roof terrace
Built
c. 1890, restored 2022–2024

An 1890s townhouse on the Graça hill, opened from cellar to roof so a single shaft of river light can travel through every floor.

The brief

The house had been divided into four rented flats in the 1970s and then left empty for eleven years. Ceilings had fallen, the stair had been boxed in, and the rear façade — the one that faces the Tagus — had been bricked down to three small windows.

Our clients, a cellist and a structural engineer returning to Lisbon after two decades in Vienna, asked for one house again: rooms for rehearsal and for grandchildren, and the view they remembered from the miradouro at the top of the street.

Our approach

We removed everything added after 1920 and kept everything before it. The original pine floor joists were repaired in place; where they could not be saved, new oak was spliced in and left visibly different, so the house can be read.

A new stair rises through the centre in a single run of Lioz limestone, lit from a roof light four storeys up. Along the river façade we reopened the four original window bays and set them deep in lime plaster, so the afternoon sun arrives softened and moves slowly across the walls.

The rehearsal room sits on the first floor where the old salon was. Its acoustic is shaped entirely by the plaster and the timber — there is no visible treatment, only a ceiling we lowered by eleven centimetres after three listening sessions with the client's quartet.
