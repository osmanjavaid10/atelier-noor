Meridian House

The library, looking west into the garden.

Location
Jumeirah, Dubai
Year
2024
Area
2,350 m²
Status
Completed
Client
Meridian Family Office
Built
1994, remade 2023–2024
Collection
212 manuscripts, 9,000 volumes

A 1990s office pavilion remade as a family office around a manuscript library that opens onto a walled garden.

The brief

The family had bought a two-storey office pavilion from 1994 — a concrete frame with mirrored glass and a car park where its garden should have been. Their previous offices were, in the founder's words, 'a bank pretending to be a living room'.

They asked for rooms where three generations could meet their advisers without anyone feeling they were in a boardroom, and a proper home for the family's collection of Arabic and Persian manuscripts.

Our approach

We stripped the building back to its frame and turned it to face inward. The car park became a walled garden of ghaf and frangipani; the mirrored glass was replaced with clear, deeply shaded glazing that looks only into it.

The library is the centre of the plan rather than a room at the end of a corridor. Its shelves rise the full double height and are lit from within, so the books glow when the garden is bright. Manuscripts are kept in climate-controlled cases at reading height, open for anyone who visits to see.

Meeting rooms are small, carpeted and named after the family's grandparents. The largest seats eight.
