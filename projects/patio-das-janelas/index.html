Pátio das Janelas

The courtyard from the fourth floor, late morning.

A 28-room hotel made from a former cork warehouse, arranged around a planted courtyard that the city cannot see.

The brief

The building stored cork for export from 1911 until the 1980s. It occupies a whole block in Madragoa, two streets from the river, with a blind courtyard at its centre that had been roofed over in corrugated sheet.

The operator wanted a small hotel for long stays — guests who arrive for a week and leave after three — with a restaurant that local people would use too.

Our approach

We took the roof off the courtyard. Everything else follows from that decision: all 28 rooms look inward onto planting and water, so the street façades could stay exactly as they were, with their iron shutters and painted numbers.

Rooms are deliberately few and generous, between 34 and 61 m². Each has a window seat built into the thickness of the old wall, and none has a television.

The restaurant occupies the former weighing hall on the ground floor. Its long table, made from the warehouse's own roof timbers, seats twenty-two.
